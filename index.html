Fabio Fuentes

I've built data-driven systems for large-scale text analysis, topic modeling, and applied econometrics. For my undergraduate thesis (advised by Benjamín Villena-Roldán), I analyzed ~60,000 Chilean news articles to estimate the dynamic effects of shocks to media narratives on subsequent economic activity.

More recently, I built a quoting system in manufacturing that encodes pricing logic into a rule-based system, logs every decision, and tracks overrides to reduce variability in pricing decisions and improve margin control.

Manufacturing Cost Model 2025
building Manufacturing Cost Modeling

A Chilean stainless steel manufacturer priced 1,300+ custom products with a single factor for decades. Building the model that replaced it: 4 complexity drivers, 11 manufacturing processes, 3 tiers each. Formalizing 30 years of one expert's intuition without disrupting his workflow.
