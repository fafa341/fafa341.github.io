LDA Topic Modeling

Latent Dirichlet Allocation discovers latent themes from word co-occurrence. Each article is a soft mix of all 10 topics, enabling temporal analysis.

NLP: Stanford Stanza

Spanish-language NLP pipeline for tokenization, lemmatization, POS tagging, and named-entity recognition — enabling clean, linguistically-aware text features.

Corpus: La Tercera + Biobío

Two leading Chilean outlets — La Tercera (national) and Biobío (digital-native) — archived via the Wayback Machine across four presidential terms.

Sentiment: BETO

Spanish-language BERT model, fine-tuned on Chilean news. Per-article sentiment aggregated to monthly resolution by dominant topic.

Signal in the Noise

A research note on quantifying collective narratives and their macroeconomic consequences.

Why let a model interpret meaning?

NLP has advanced from bag-of-words frequency counts to models that approximate contextual understanding without explicit symbolic reasoning. LLMs don't "understand" in the human sense — but they can model statistical regularities in meaning that are invisible to earlier methods like TF-IDF or LDA alone.

By combining unsupervised topic structure (LDA), fine-tuned sentiment (BETO), and contextual interpretation (LLM), this framework chains three increasingly expressive representations of the same signal. Each layer adds signal the previous one cannot capture. This is the direction the field is moving: not replacing human interpretation, but making it falsifiable and scalable.

Three analytical layers

Layer 1
Representational
Statistical associations, embeddings, topic structure. What topics exist and how do they co-occur?
Layer 2
Latent Interpretive
Framing, intent, moral stance, power dynamics. What is being said and how is it framed?
Layer 3
Behavioral Consequence
Attention allocation, collective coordination, belief shifts. What did people do as a result?

Combining these layers with substantive domain knowledge helps uncover causal and interpretive insights in the behavioral sciences — moving beyond surface-level fluency toward measurable social impact.

Benchmarking & next steps

We benchmarked the narrative signal against IMACEC — Chile's broad monthly activity index. This confirms the engine works. The real edge lies in sectoral decomposition: matching Topic 8 (Mining & Resources) to the copper sub-index, Topic 3 (Monetary Policy) to Banco Central rate decisions, Topic 7 (Investment & Energy) to FDI data. Tighter identification reduces omitted variable bias and sharpens the causal signal toward valid inference.

The longer-run research agenda evaluates guided, supervised, and unsupervised models alongside LLMs as components in a single modeling stack — not in isolation. This allows a clearer analysis of accuracy, time, and cost trade-offs under realistic constraints, where accuracy includes topic manipulation robustness, semantic consistency over time, and contextual stability.
