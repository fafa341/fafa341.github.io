LDA Topic Modeling

Latent Dirichlet Allocation discovers latent themes from word co-occurrence. Each article is a soft mix of all 10 topics, enabling temporal analysis.

Corpus: La Tercera

Chile's leading newspaper, economics section, archived via the Wayback Machine. Covers nearly the full Chilean business-cycle period studied.

Sentiment: BETO

Spanish-language BERT model, fine-tuned on Chilean news. Per-article sentiment aggregated to monthly resolution by dominant topic.

Macro Correlation

Monthly IMACEC and unemployment from Chile's Banco Central, overlaid to test whether discourse shifts lead macroeconomic conditions.
