Latent Dirichlet Allocation discovers latent themes from word co-occurrence. Each article is a soft mix of all 10 topics, enabling temporal analysis.

NLP: Stanford Stanza

Spanish-language NLP pipeline for tokenization, lemmatization, POS tagging, and named-entity recognition enabling clean, linguistically-aware text features.

Spanish-language BERT model, fine-tuned on Chilean news. Per-article sentiment aggregated to monthly resolution by dominant topic.

Limitations & Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to the questions a researcher or reviewer would ask. Knowing what a method cannot do is as important as knowing what it can.

NLPWhy K=10 topics? Could a different number change the findings? ▼

K=10 was selected after training and inspecting models across K=5 to K=15. The criterion was interpretability: the configuration that produced the clearest thematic separation and the most economically coherent clusters. No single formal coherence metric was used as the deciding criterion this is standard practice in applied LDA work, where human validation of topic meaning matters as much as statistical scores.

The implication: the specific topic boundaries are analytical choices, not objective facts. At K=8, Monetary Policy and Macroeconomic Outlook might merge; at K=15, Government & Politics might split into executive and legislative sub-topics. The main econometric results focus on the Monetary Policy narrative, which consistently emerges as a distinct cluster across the evaluated range. The broader discourse structure its shifts across elections, crises, and reforms is robust to reasonable variation in K.

EconomicsDoes this show that media narratives cause economic outcomes? ▼

Not in a strictly structural sense. The econometric model is a state-dependent Local Projection (Jordà 2005; Ramey & Zubairy 2018), which establishes that narrative shocks at time t contain information about IMACEC outcomes at t+h not already captured by lags of the outcome itself a Granger-causal relationship. The state variable is lagged one period to ensure predetermination, and placebo tests with randomly shuffled shocks confirm the results are not spurious.

The reverse channel that economic conditions drive media coverage is equally plausible and not fully excluded. A structural causal claim would require an instrument or a natural experiment that shifts media narratives independently of underlying economic fundamentals. The correct interpretation is: narrative shifts contain predictive information about future economic activity, consistent with theories of expectation formation and Rational Inattention, but the causal direction is not fully identified.

DataDo two outlets represent Chilean economic discourse? ▼

La Tercera and Biobío were selected from 14 candidate outlets on the basis of archive consistency and publication frequency both maintained near-daily snapshots on the Wayback Machine across the full 2012–2024 window, a requirement for a valid monthly time series. La Tercera covers a centre-right, business-oriented readership; Biobío is a digital-native outlet with a broader urban audience.

Left-leaning outlets (El Mostrador) and the dominant conservative business press (El Mercurio) are not included. The topic proportions and sentiment baseline therefore reflect the editorial priorities of these two specific outlets. Whether the results generalise to a more ideologically diverse corpus remains an open question. For a small open economy with a concentrated media market, however, the two outlets cover the majority of Chilean online economic news readership during this period.

NLPAre the sentiment scores economically meaningful, or just linguistic? ▼

BETO was fine-tuned on a general Spanish-language sentiment dataset, not on financial or economic news specifically. The model captures linguistic tone whether the language is positive or negative in a general sense rather than economic valence. These can diverge: a technically-phrased article about a rate hike might read as neutral while being economically contractionary; a report on rising inflation might use alarmed language while describing conditions that were anticipated by markets.

The sentiment variable should be interpreted as a proxy for the negative register of economic coverage how worried or pessimistic the language sounds not as a direct measure of economic optimism. Domain-specific fine-tuning on labelled Chilean financial articles would sharpen this distinction and is identified as a priority for future work.

MethodLDA destroys word order. Doesn't that matter for economics? ▼

Yes and it is the central limitation of the topic modeling layer. LDA is a bag-of-words model: syntax, word order, and negation are discarded. "Banco Central no subió tasas" and "Banco Central sí subió tasas" produce identical feature representations. Direction rates up or down, growth accelerating or slowing is invisible at the LDA stage.

This is why the BETO sentiment layer matters: it recovers the directional signal that LDA cannot. The two layers operate on different dimensions. LDA answers what is being discussed; BETO answers how it is being discussed. Together they capture the structure and tone of discourse but neither fully captures propositional content in the way a modern contextual embedding model (e.g., a fine-tuned transformer) would. BERTopic or neural topic models would be a natural next step.

MethodThe year interpretations are AI-generated how much should I trust them? ▼

The year-by-year insights were generated offline using Claude Haiku (Anthropic), with each year's actual topic proportions, average sentiment, and key events as explicit inputs. They are pre-computed static content no live API calls are made when you browse the site.

Important caveat: the model has background knowledge of Chilean history. The interpretations integrate the quantitative corpus data with the model's prior knowledge of what happened in Chile between 2012 and 2024. They should be read as contextualised synthesis a coherent narrative that connects the measured discourse structure to known historical events not as independent empirical discoveries from the data alone. They are illustrative, not inferential.

Why let a model interpret meaning?

NLP has advanced from bag-of-words frequency counts to models that approximate contextual understanding without explicit symbolic reasoning. LLMs don't "understand" in the human sense but they can model statistical regularities in meaning that are invisible to earlier methods like TF-IDF or LDA alone.

By combining unsupervised topic structure (LDA), fine-tuned sentiment (BETO), and contextual interpretation (LLM), this framework chains three increasingly expressive representations of the same signal. Each layer adds signal the previous one cannot capture. This is the direction the field is moving: not replacing human interpretation, but making it falsifiable and scalable.

Three analytical layers

Layer 1
Representational
Statistical associations, embeddings, topic structure. What topics exist and how do they co-occur?
Layer 2
Latent Interpretive
Framing, intent, moral stance, power dynamics. What is being said and how is it framed?
Layer 3
Behavioral Consequence
Attention allocation, collective coordination, belief shifts. What did people do as a result?

Combining these layers with substantive domain knowledge helps uncover causal and interpretive insights in the behavioral sciences moving beyond surface-level fluency toward measurable social impact.

Benchmarking & next steps

We benchmarked the narrative signal against IMACEC Chile's broad monthly activity index. This confirms the engine works. The real edge lies in sectoral decomposition: matching Topic 8 (Mining & Resources) to the copper sub-index, Topic 3 (Monetary Policy) to Banco Central rate decisions, Topic 7 (Investment & Energy) to FDI data. Tighter identification reduces omitted variable bias and sharpens the causal signal toward valid inference.

The longer-run research agenda evaluates guided, supervised, and unsupervised models alongside LLMs as components in a single modeling stack not in isolation. This allows a clearer analysis of accuracy, time, and cost trade-offs under realistic constraints, where accuracy includes topic manipulation robustness, semantic consistency over time, and contextual stability.
